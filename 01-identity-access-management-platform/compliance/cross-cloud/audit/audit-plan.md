# IAM Compliance Audit Plan

## Scope

AWS accounts, Azure subscriptions and tenant identities, and Google Cloud organization, folders, and projects represented in the lab.

## Procedures

1. Confirm the authoritative identity inventory.
2. Validate privileged-user MFA.
3. Sample role assignments and confirm approval evidence.
4. Review inactive users and credentials.
5. Review service identities for owner, purpose, and credential type.
6. Review external trust and guest access.
7. Confirm audit logging coverage.
8. Validate break-glass controls.
9. Recalculate control status from collected evidence.
10. Record exceptions and remediation due dates.

## Sampling

For each provider, examine all privileged identities and a risk-based sample of standard identities, groups, roles, and service identities.

## Evidence Retention

Evidence should be retained according to organizational policy. Lab evidence in this repository is retained for portfolio demonstration and contains no secrets.
