# Microsoft Entra Conditional Access Standard

## Policies

1. Require MFA for administrators.
2. Require MFA for all interactive workforce access.
3. Block legacy authentication.
4. Require compliant or approved devices for sensitive applications.
5. Apply sign-in risk controls where licensing permits.
6. Protect security information registration.
7. Exclude only designated emergency access accounts.
8. Monitor exclusions and policy changes.

## Deployment Method

Policies are introduced in report-only mode, reviewed for impact, tested with pilot groups, and then enforced.

## Emergency Access

Two cloud-only emergency accounts are maintained with strong credentials, dedicated monitoring, and no routine use.
