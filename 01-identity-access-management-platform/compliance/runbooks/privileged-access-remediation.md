# Runbook: Excessive Privileged Access

## Trigger

A user, group, role, or service identity has privileges beyond approved responsibilities.

## Procedure

1. Preserve the current policy or role assignment as evidence.
2. Identify the principal, owner, business purpose, scope, and last activity.
3. Determine whether active misuse is suspected.
4. If misuse is suspected, revoke sessions and escalate to incident response.
5. Remove the broad assignment or replace it with a narrower role.
6. Validate that required business functions still work.
7. Capture the corrected policy and assessment result.
8. Update the risk register and close the finding after independent review.

## Rollback

Restore only the minimum prior permission required to recover a documented business function. Record the rollback reason and obtain owner approval.
