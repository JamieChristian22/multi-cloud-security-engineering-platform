# Runbook: Inactive or Orphaned Identity

1. Confirm last authentication and last resource use.
2. Identify the manager, application owner, or service owner.
3. Check for dependencies and automation.
4. Disable interactive access or credentials.
5. Monitor for failed dependency activity.
6. Remove access after the retention period.
7. Preserve the decision and evidence.
8. Escalate identities without owners as governance risks.
