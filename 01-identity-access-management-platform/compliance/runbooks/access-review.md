# Runbook: Quarterly Access Review

1. Export users, groups, roles, service identities, and privileged assignments.
2. Reconcile each identity to an accountable owner.
3. Identify inactive, external, orphaned, and highly privileged identities.
4. Send review decisions to business owners.
5. Remove rejected or expired access.
6. Validate changes through fresh exports.
7. Hash and archive the evidence.
8. Record completion metrics and unresolved exceptions.
