# CIS-Aligned IAM Security Baseline

This baseline is intentionally provider-neutral and avoids claiming formal certification.

## Required Controls

1. Root, owner, and global administrator accounts use MFA.
2. Daily administration uses named accounts rather than shared accounts.
3. Long-lived access keys are avoided for workforce identities.
4. Inactive credentials are disabled.
5. Privileged access is separated from normal user access.
6. Administrative actions are logged.
7. Direct user grants are replaced with group or role assignments.
8. Public or external trust relationships are reviewed.
9. Service identities have documented owners and purposes.
10. Access reviews occur at least quarterly.
11. Break-glass identities are monitored and tested.
12. Policy changes require peer review.
